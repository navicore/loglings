# Hint — 01-univ-build

`=..` with the term on the LEFT and a list on the RIGHT builds the term.
The worked example showed it as a query (`T =.. [greet, world]`); here you
wrap that single goal in a rule, with the functor and the two arguments
arriving as parameters.

One goal in the body — let `=..` assemble the term; you don't build it by
hand.
