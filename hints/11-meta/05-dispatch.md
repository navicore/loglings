# Hint — 05-dispatch

Two goals, the two builtins this chapter taught, in order. First turn the
name and the two arguments into a real goal with `=..` (remember the list
is `[Functor | Args]`). Then hand that goal to `call` to run it.

The worked example showed the exact shape on a one-argument command; yours
has two arguments instead of one. The name chooses the predicate, `=..`
makes it a goal, `call` executes it.
